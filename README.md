# LSAT Arena

Static ranked Logical Reasoning duel site. Open `index.html`, or visit https://dovigertelman-dot.github.io/lsat-arena/.

Ranked duels and the daily challenge are five original LR questions. Weak spots, friend challenges, Play vs AI, and Beat your best can be 5, 15, 30, or 50, with an optional section timer at about 1:25 per question. Accuracy decides; speed breaks ties. Rating, record, pattern stats, badges, the daily streak, and past runs stay in this browser.

- Play vs AI offers Easy, Medium, and Hard. Easy is right about half the time and slow, Medium about 70%, and Hard about 88% and fast. Hard gets sharper on the harder questions; Easy gets shakier.
- Beat your best races a ghost of your best saved run, or any chosen past run, at the same length and type. The opponent bar is that run's pace. Score and time are compared at the end.
- Online is a disabled card marked coming soon. Live matches are not open.

- Ranked duels open with a 3, 2, 1, Go countdown. Tap an answer to select it, tap again to submit, or tap × to clear it.
- The bank has 585 original questions across the main LR types, tagged with pattern and difficulty. About 30% are easy, 40% medium, and 30% hard. Nothing is taken from a PrepTest.
- Pattern stats track accuracy and average time, including by question type. Train weak spots draws an unduplicated set from your lowest patterns.
- The daily challenge is the same five questions for everyone on a UTC date, with a streak.
- Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Diamond follow your Elo. Badges mark a first ranked win, a 5-win streak, a perfect 5/5, and a 7-day daily streak.
- Challenge a friend with a link that replays your questions and compares the scores. Finished sets are saved in this browser so you can race them later.
- The page is a cream layout with soft blue, a warm accent, rounded cards, and a faint dot pattern. Rank and the daily streak stay visible. Sound starts muted. Dark mode is in the header.
